# GP Games

Games about life in UK general practice.

## Last Partner Standing

A survival game about one QOF year, April to March, as a new GP partner. Each month you plan your sessions, locum cover, staff and one project, then deal with whatever lands on your desk: patients, the team, the ICB, CQC and a leaking flat roof. Keep Patients, Team, You and Safety above zero and the bank above −£40k, and at year end the accountant tells you what it was all worth.

**Play:** download [`last-partner-standing/last-partner-standing-play.html`](last-partner-standing/last-partner-standing-play.html) and open it in any browser. It's a single file with nothing to install.

- Three practices: leafy suburb (gentle), market town (standard) and inner city (brutal)
- About 90 decision cards and story arcs, including:
  - a retiring senior partner who wants his share of the building
  - a partner with a laminated job advert for Perth
  - a salaried GP who wants partnership
  - a corporate takeover offer
  - a CQC inspection
- Two mini-games:
  - Docman Dash: file, action, urgent or bounce
  - The 8am Rush: route requests to 999, GP, routine, Pharmacy First or physio
- Year-end accounts, with QOF reconciled against the aspiration payments, and an ending title

The practices, people and companies are fictional and the scenarios are satire. Figures are tuned for play, not taken from the GP contract.
